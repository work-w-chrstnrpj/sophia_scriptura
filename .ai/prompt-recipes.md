# Sophia prompt recipes

The maintained command guide is
[How we develop Sophia](../docs/guidelines/ai-structure-guidelines.md).

- Explain: “Use Mentor to walk me through the roadmap and what SP-006 proves.”
- Plan: “Plan the native Pencil toolbar and write a ticket. Do not implement yet.”
- Review: “Review SP-006's architecture for maintainability. Report findings without edits.”
- Execute: “Execute SP-006 within its agreed scope and record verification.”
- Test: “Guide me through the SP-006 iPad walkthrough, one check at a time.”
- Document: “Update the ticket with my feedback and recommend the next step.”

Choose a role and skill from `.agents/` as appropriate. A recommendation or feedback
request does not start another implementation ticket.
