# API.Bible complete offline editions — request draft

Status: prepared for owner review; not sent. Do not include an API key when sending.
Recipient: support@api.bible
Subject: Complete offline Bible storage for a single-user personal iPad app

Hello API.Bible team,

I am developing Sophia Scriptura, a native iPad Bible-study app used only by me on my
personal device in the Philippines. It is not distributed through the App Store and has
no sales, ads, subscriptions, fundraising or other monetization. My account exposes:

- NIV11 — 78a9f6124f344018-01
- NLT — d6e14a625393b4da-01
- NKJV — 63097d2a0a2f7db3-01

I would like to download each complete edition once into protected local app storage,
read it without a network connection, and perform the required refresh at least every
30 days. I would preserve text, source structure, edition identity, publisher attribution
and the API.Bible link; copies would not be published or exported as Bible databases.

Section 8 of your terms restricts systematic extraction to populate a database, whereas
section 11 describes offline storage. Can you confirm in writing whether a complete
on-device download for this specific personal use is permitted? If it requires additional
permission, please identify the process or an approved full-edition download mechanism.
Please also specify any storage-size limits, protection requirements, refresh protocol,
and required behavior if I remain offline beyond 30 days.

The app's longer-term plan includes an AI study companion, which is not implemented.
I would not send these licensed texts to AI or enable text-to-speech without appropriate
permission. Because Biblica's express-license criteria concern AI functionality at the
application level, please clarify whether NIV can coexist with that future feature and
what additional approval would be needed. I am not assuming a text-only guard is sufficient.

Please also clarify permitted personal verse quotations in notes, Copy/Share limits,
and whether the proposed offline permission applies to all three edition IDs above.

Thank you.
