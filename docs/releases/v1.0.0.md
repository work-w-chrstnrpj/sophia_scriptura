# Sophia Scriptura v1.0.0

Release date: **2026-10-13**. App version **1.0.0**, build **22**.

## Scripture and narration

Adds source-neutral translation identities and the Hello AO catalog, parser, generation store and offline edition repository alongside bundled WEBP and API.Bible. The selected AO editions are KJV, BSB, ASV, LSV and WEBU, with WEBU retaining its additional-book inventory. Translation selection and download management now have separate screens; Settings opens downloads and API.Bible sync separately. The licensed picker lists NASB 1995, NLT and NKJV; legacy NIV references retain their identity.

BSB narration adds a player, verse timing/highlighting, narrator selection, chapter/seek controls, independent Follow and Continuous preferences, and explicit chapter/book audio download management. Narration and background music share coordinated playback ownership. Playback coverage and highlighting depend on available source assets and matching text/timing metadata.

Sophy gains folder intent, Scripture navigation and slash-command presentation refinements. Activity includes reading-end suggestions and revised workflow presentation. Reader text extraction, appearance, prayer editing and Settings presentation receive related fixes.
