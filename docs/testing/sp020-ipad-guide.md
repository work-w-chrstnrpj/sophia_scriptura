# SP-020 Activity iPad walkthrough

Status: not run on the owner's physical iPad. Record observed results in the SP-020 ticket.

Use a test Activity archive before changing personal records. Restore it afterward if needed.

1. In Activity, start Reading from a verse in the current edition. Confirm Scripture opens, the verse focus appears briefly, and annotations remain untouched.
2. Change tabs while the timer runs. Pause from the shared control, wait, resume and finish from Activity. Choose an ending verse in another chapter or book. Confirm the saved duration excludes the pause and history retains the inclusive range.
3. Start another timer, lock the screen, background Sophia, then relaunch. Confirm the same draft and a timestamp-derived duration; paused time must not accrue.
4. Add a manual activity with title, mode, start date/time and minutes; confirm the form previews the derived end and the save succeeds. Add another using start and end; confirm the duration is derived. Add an overlapping interval and confirm it is rejected. Edit a timed record, confirm its original start/end are prefilled, change them and save; verify there is still one record with the corrected time and Today/Analytics recalculate.
5. Create and archive a custom mode. Confirm earlier history retains its label and can still be corrected.
6. Complete a timed and an untimed Quiz Me session through the final summary, without starting an Activity timer. Confirm each creates one Reviewing entry with active question time and no quiz answer/score copy. Abandon a quiz and confirm it creates none. Delete one completed quiz from Quiz history and confirm its Reviewing time remains in Activity.
7. Export the Activity archive through Files, change a test record, then restore the archive with confirmation. Confirm restored records and goal.
8. Inspect the shared control on Scripture, Notes, Companion and Activity with the keyboard and Pencil tools shown. Confirm no controls or writing area are blocked.
9. In light and dark appearance, inspect Activity in portrait and landscape. Confirm the paper background reaches both side edges, and Analytics, Today and History use the same palette as the other tabs.
10. Change Analytics between Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Annual. Compare pie distribution and bar minutes with saved records, then select one mode and confirm both charts filter together. Check a manual record without clock times appears under **No time** in Daily.
11. Confirm Today shows a current streak of at least 1 on first use. Create more than five records; verify the preview shows only the latest five and **View all** opens the full history in relative date groups. Tap a row for its details, then swipe a row to reveal Edit and red Delete.
12. Drag the cross-tab recorder to several positions and switch tabs. Collapse it to check that only the mode and timer icon remain; expand it to check seconds and pause/finish icons. Finish from another tab and confirm the final review sheet opens on Activity.
13. Drag the floating recorder continuously across the screen; it should stay under the finger with no initial pause or trailing animation. Tap its buttons immediately afterward to confirm dragging did not block taps.
14. Tap the Analytics date/range for each period. Pick a day, then a date in a different week, month and year. Confirm Weekly uses the chosen date's full calendar week, and both charts follow the chosen range and mode. Confirm the pie/time cards occupy about 40%/60% of the row.
15. In the Reminders section after History, use New to create Once, Daily, Weekdays, Weekends and Weekly reminders. Confirm each successful save closes the form, the section previews five items, View all groups every reminder, and rows open for editing with swipe Edit/Delete. Confirm the X closes the form and tapping outside does not. Test delivery at a near-future time with Sophia open and closed. Select Delete after it fires on one one-time reminder and leave it off on another; after both fire, reopen Sophia and confirm only the selected reminder is removed.
16. Select a long Daily or Monthly time chart and scroll it horizontally. Use Print Analytics in the Analytics header, save the PNG through Files, and inspect that the full bar axis is included along with the selected date/range and mode. Confirm Auxiliary has neither Reminder nor Print Analytics.
17. Open a history record and inspect the compact detail layout in light and dark modes. Confirm Edit has readable contrast and Delete is red. Confirm Activity, Chat and Quiz history swipe Delete actions are red.
18. Tap Activity distribution. Confirm its card keeps the pie and color/activity legend without times, and its detail sheet shows each type's duration and share. Confirm the selected period and type filter carry through.
19. Tap Today. Confirm the card has the date, total minutes and one progress line, with Start Activity floating above the tab selector at bottom right. In the detail sheet inspect each mode's progress, then use the bottom-right chevron to expand goal editing. Enter different goals, save, and confirm the total and per-mode progress update after relaunch. Check that all-zero or over-1,440-minute totals are rejected without changing saved goals.

Simulator checks cover only the model and file behavior; this walkthrough establishes device and visual acceptance.
