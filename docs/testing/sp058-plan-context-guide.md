# SP-058 personal iPad checks

Use an existing Plan with Lady Wisdom enabled and the preferred provider configured.
Automated fixtures do not establish real-provider interpretation quality.

1. Open the Plan and start a Day. Verify both the Activity timer and Lady Wisdom are
   visible. Expand the timer, pause/resume, drag it, and rotate the iPad. Reading and
   verse selection should work outside the controls. Finish should leave the Plan and
   reuse the existing Activity finish flow; unfinished Days must not enter History.
2. Edit a Plan, choose **Add multiple sections…**, enter **6** and choose Add sections.
   Done, exit, reopen, and confirm six additional numbered sections and their folders.
   Invalid/zero/over-capacity counts must leave the previous sections intact.
3. Tap the Plan header's Share with Lady Wisdom, or tap the character then Share this
   Plan. Confirm the attachment title and provider disclosure appear before sending.
4. Send “Add 15 more days to this Plan.” Inspect the before/after counts. Cancel once
   and verify no change. Ask again and Confirm. Reopen and inspect the extra sections.
5. Share again and ask “Make 60 the total days of this Plan.” Confirm only the missing
   sections are added; existing references, titles and completed items remain intact.
6. Share and ask “Add Genesis 1:1 and 1 Kings 1:1 to Day 2.” Preview must list both
   references under Day 2. Confirm, reopen, and inspect both Scripture items in its folder.
   Repeat across specified Days. Without a destination, Sophy should ask which Day.
7. While a preview is open, cancel it or alter the Plan and re-share. An old preview
   must never overwrite the newer Plan. Removing the attachment starts a fresh chat.
8. Protected Plans/folders cannot be shared. Backgrounding clears attached context.
   No note text, note titles, link bodies, credentials or Bible wording should be included.
9. Check Sophy History for Updated Plan and the new sections/references. A successful
   change requires a new share before the next Plan edit; no change happens on Send alone.

Record device/OS, provider/model, observed outcomes and any layout or interpretation issue.
